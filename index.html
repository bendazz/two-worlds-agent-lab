Your agent, and two worlds it has never heard of

You'll build one agent with three tools: a search tool for each of two invented history books, plus a calculator. Then you'll ask it the questions on the next tab and score two things for each one: did it get the answer right, and did it take the right route to get there, meaning the tools it called.

How scoring works

  1. Ask the agent a question. Use Copy to paste it into the Langflow Playground exactly.
  2. Record what the trace shows: which tools it called and how many model calls it made.
  3. Check against the key. The expected answer and route appear, and the route is scored for you.
  4. Mark the answer ✓ correct, ½ partial or ✗ wrong.

The key stays hidden until you've recorded the trace. Please don't peek first. The point is to see what your agent does. Your work saves in this browser as you go.

Studio rubric

Each dimension is scored 0 (weak), 1 (okay) or 2 (strong). The first four come from your scores: 2 is 80% or more, 1 is 50–79%, 0 is below 50%. You score Cost & steps yourself.
