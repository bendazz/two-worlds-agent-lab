Getting around

  • Nobody flies. People cross between islands on rope bridges or in cable-barges.
  • No bridge reaches Orrin's Shelf. The only way there is the Long Tether.
  • Orrin's Shelf is the farming island. Orrin's Step is the prison rock beneath it. Don't mix them up.
  • Kestra can be crossed from its western brink to its eastern brink in one long day.

Not on this map

  • Rivers. The Drift has none. Water comes from rain cisterns.
  • What lies beneath the clouds. No one in the Drift looks over the edge, so no one knows.
