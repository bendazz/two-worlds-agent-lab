For the traveler

  • Varn is the marsh town in the far east. Varne is the island city in the River Sidd. Don't mix them up.
  • The Sidd flows south, from Ost to the sea. It forks at Lanterncross into the Greater and the Lesser Sidd.
  • Crossing the Vale on the Harvest Road takes two full days. The Causeway of Stakes from Fenwatch to Varn takes one day.
  • The Saltings coast has no harbor, and the Saltings keep no navy.

All places and distances come from A Traveler's History of the Mereholt. Where the book gives no exact position, a place is drawn in the right region only.
